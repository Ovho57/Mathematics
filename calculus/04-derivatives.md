# 04 — Derivatives: Meaning and Definition

A derivative measures **instantaneous rate of change** and gives the slope of a tangent line.

## From average rate to instantaneous rate

Average rate:
\[
\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}h
\]

Let (h) approach zero:
\[
f'(x)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}h
\]

This is the derivative definition.

## Derivation: (f(x)=x^2)

\[
f'(x)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{(x+h)^2-x^2}h
\]
Expand:
\[
=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{2xh+h^2}h
=\lim_{h\to0}(2x+h)=2x
\]

So (f'(x)=2x). At (x=3), slope (=6).

## Tangent line

At (x=a):
\[
y-f(a)=f'(a)(x-a)
\]

For (y=x^2) at (x=2), the point is ((2,4)) and slope is (4):
\[
y-4=4(x-2)\Rightarrow y=4x-4
\]

## Notation

(f'(x)), (dy/dx), and (d f/dx) commonly represent the derivative.
