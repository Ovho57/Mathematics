# 10 — Further Calculus: Series and Multivariable Ideas

This is a bridge to advanced calculus. Learn limits, derivatives, and integrals first.

## Sequences

A sequence is an ordered list (a_1,a_2,\ldots). Example (a_n=1/n) approaches zero as (n\to\infty).

## Series

A series adds terms:
\[
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n
\]

Terms approaching zero is necessary for convergence, but not sufficient.

## Taylor approximation

Near (x=a), a function may be approximated by:
\[
f(x)\approx f(a)+f'(a)(x-a)+\frac{f''(a)}2(x-a)^2
\]

This is useful for local approximations when conditions are satisfied.

## Partial derivatives

For (f(x,y)), a partial derivative measures change in one variable while holding the other fixed.

\[
f(x,y)=x^2+3y\Rightarrow f_x=2x
\]

## Gradient

\[
\nabla f=(f_x,f_y)
\]

The gradient points in the direction of steepest increase for differentiable functions.

## Practice

Find both partial derivatives of (f(x,y)=x^2y+4y).

**Answer:** (f_x=2xy), (f_y=x^2+4).
