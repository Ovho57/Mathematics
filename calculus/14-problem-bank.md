# 14 — Mixed Calculus Problem Bank

Attempt every question first and show full working.

## Foundation

1. Evaluate (lim_{x\to2}(3x+1)).
2. Differentiate (x^4-3x^2+2).
3. Integrate (2x+5).

## Core

4. Differentiate ((2x+1)^5).
5. Find the tangent line to (y=x^2+1) at (x=2).
6. Evaluate (int_0^3 2x,dx).
7. Find and classify the stationary point of (x^2-6x+8).

## Applications

8. Position is (s(t)=t^3-2t). Find velocity and acceleration.
9. Find maximum area for a rectangle with perimeter 40.
10. Rate is (r(t)=4t) litres/min. Find accumulation from (t=0) to (5).

## Challenge

11. Evaluate (int2x(x^2+3)^4dx).
12. Solve (dy/dt=3y), (y(0)=7).

## Answers

1. (7)
2. (4x^3-6x)
3. (x^2+5x+C)
4. (10(2x+1)^4)
5. (y'=2x); slope 4 at (2,5): (y=4x-3)
6. ([x^2]_0^3=9)
7. (f'=2x-6=0\Rightarrow x=3); (f(3)=-1); (f''=2>0), minimum at ((3,-1))
8. (v=3t^2-2), (a=6t)
9. Square of side 10; area 100
10. (int_0^5 4t,dt=50) litres
11. Substitution (u=x^2+3): ((x^2+3)^5/5+C)
12. (y=7e^{3t})
