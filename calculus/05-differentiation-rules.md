# 05 — Differentiation Rules

## Constant and power rules

\[
\frac{d}{dx}(c)=0,\qquad \frac{d}{dx}x^n=nx^{n-1}
\]

Example: ((x^5)'=5x^4).

## Sum and constant multiple

\[
(cf)'=cf',\quad (f+g)'=f'+g'
\]

Example:
\[
(3x^4-2x^2+7)'=12x^3-4x
\]

## Product rule

\[
(fg)'=f'g+fg'
\]

## Quotient rule

\[
\left(\frac fg\right)'=\frac{f'g-fg'}{g^2}
\]

## Chain rule

For a function inside another function:
\[
(f(g(x)))'=f'(g(x))g'(x)
\]

Example:
\[
((3x+1)^4)'=4(3x+1)^3\cdot3=12(3x+1)^3
\]

## Common derivatives

\[
(\sin x)'=\cos x,\quad(\cos x)'=-\sin x
\]
\[
(e^x)'=e^x,\quad(\ln x)'=1/x
\]

Trigonometric derivative formulas use radians.

## Exam process

Identify the rule, write it, substitute, simplify, and check the domain.
