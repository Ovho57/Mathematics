# 06 — Applications of Derivatives

## Increasing and decreasing

- (f'(x)>0): function is increasing on the interval.
- (f'(x)<0): function is decreasing.

## Stationary points

Solve (f'(x)=0). A stationary point may be a maximum, minimum, or neither.

Second derivative test:
- (f''(x)>0): local minimum (under the usual test conditions).
- (f''(x)<0): local maximum.
- (f''(x)=0): test inconclusive.

## Optimization checklist

1. Define variables.
2. Write the quantity to optimize.
3. Use constraints to reduce to one variable.
4. Differentiate.
5. Set derivative equal to zero.
6. Check endpoints and whether it is truly optimal.
7. Give units and interpret the result.

## Example: rectangle with perimeter 20

\[
2x+2y=20\Rightarrow y=10-x
\]
\[
A=x(10-x)=10x-x^2
\]
\[
A'=10-2x=0\Rightarrow x=5
\]
Thus (y=5), and maximum area is (25) square units.

## Motion

If position is (s(t)):
\[
v(t)=s'(t),\quad a(t)=s''(t)
\]

Velocity may be negative because direction matters.
