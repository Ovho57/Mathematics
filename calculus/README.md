# Calculus — From Zero to Expert

Calculus is the mathematics of **change** and **accumulation**. This folder is a step-by-step textbook for learners who find calculus difficult. It starts with the foundations and moves toward advanced ideas, exam questions, and real-world modelling.

## What calculus means in everyday language

- **Limit:** What value are we getting closer to?
- **Derivative:** How fast is something changing at this instant?
- **Integral:** How much has accumulated over a period?
- **Differential equation:** What mathematical rule describes how something changes?

## Study routine

For each lesson, read the intuition first, then the definition. Cover the worked solution and try it yourself. Do the practice questions on paper. Check your answer only after attempting the problem. Write down why mistakes happened and redo those problems later.
