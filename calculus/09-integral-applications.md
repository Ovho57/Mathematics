# 09 — Applications of Integrals

## Area under a curve

If (f(x)\ge0):
\[
A=\int_a^b f(x)dx
\]

If the curve crosses the axis, split the interval and use absolute values for geometric area.

## Area between curves

If (f) is above (g):
\[
A=\int_a^b(f(x)-g(x))dx
\]

Find intersections and determine which curve is above.

## Volume of revolution: disks

For a region revolved around the x-axis:
\[
V=\pi\int_a^b[f(x)]^2dx
\]

The radius is the distance to the axis.

## Accumulation from a rate

If (r(t)) is a rate:
\[
\Delta Q=\int_a^b r(t)dt
\]

Example: 5 litres/minute for 3 minutes gives 15 litres.

## Units

Always check units: area is square units, volume cubic units, and integrating a rate over time produces the accumulated quantity.

## Practice

A constant rate of 8 units/minute lasts 4 minutes. How much accumulates?

**Answer:** 32 units.
